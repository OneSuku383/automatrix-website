Who We Are

AutoMatrix Technology is a Malaysian engineering company dedicated to helping startups, SMEs, and industrial manufacturers build reliable hardware and automate complex business workflows.

By combining industrial automation, manufacturing system design, and bespoke software development under one roof, we take projects from initial concept to deployment-ready systems.

Our Vision

To be a trusted and innovative automation engineering company, driving industrial transformation through intelligent and integrated technology solutions.

Our Mission

To develop reliable, efficient and innovative automation systems by integrating electrical, mechanical and control engineering technologies while delivering quality solutions that improve productivity and support industrial advancement.

What We Do

Comprehensive Engineering Services

Integrated solutions spanning supply and maintenance, manufacturing system design, industrial automation, and software.

Supply & Maintenance Services

Supply of automation components, sensors, drives, and spare parts, plus preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair to keep your production running.

Manufacturing System Design

Production line and process layout design, conveyor and material-handling systems, and integration of machines, controls, and data for efficient manufacturing.

Industrial Automation & Control

Control Panel Design & Fabrication

Machine & Mechanical Design

Embedded & Software Automation
